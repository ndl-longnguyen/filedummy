Cloud storage pricing consists of three components: **Storage** (per GB/month), **Operations** (PUT, GET requests), and **Data Egress** (bandwidth transferred out to the internet). For download-heavy platforms, egress frequently accounts for over 80% of the total infrastructure cost.

|----------|----------------|----------------------|----------------|-----------------|

## Những Điểm Cốt Lõi Cần Ghi Nhớ
- **The Egress Trap:** Amazon S3 charges $0.09/GB for data transfer out. Serving just 100TB of files to your users costs $9,000 every month on AWS.
- **Why R2 Wins:** Cloudflare does not charge for egress bandwidth. You only pay $0.015/GB/month for data stored and standard per-operation fees ($0.36 per million Class B read operations).
